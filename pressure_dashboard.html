Pressure / usual level = runs above league per 100 balls in the same situation (opposition and venue adjusted, shrunk toward the player's own baseline). Pos. inn % = share of pressure innings with a positive win-probability contribution. P(top 10) = bootstrap chance of staying in the top 10 among available players. Re-weighting uses the 20%-pressure cut-off components for the top 30 only; the official list is the 3-cut-off ensemble (weights 0.45 / 0.20 / 0.20 / 0.15).
